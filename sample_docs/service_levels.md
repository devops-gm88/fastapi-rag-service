# Service levels

Support hours are 09:00 to 17:00 Eastern Time, Monday to Friday.

The first response target is 4 business hours.

The monthly cost cap is CAD 2,400. The contract term is 3 years.

No uptime percentage is guaranteed in this phase. The pilot measures availability and reports it monthly.

## Escalation

An unacknowledged alert escalates after 20 minutes. The escalation goes to the depot supervisor first, then the fleet manager.
